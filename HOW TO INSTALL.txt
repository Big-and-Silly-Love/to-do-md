TO DO APP - HOW TO INSTALL AND UPDATE
=====================================

WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER
  index.html, manifest.json, sw.js, icon-180.png, icon-192.png, icon-512.png  -> the app
  todo.md   -> your to-do list (a plain text file you can edit right on GitHub)
  Excel version (optional)/todo.xlsx -> only if you'd rather keep the list in Excel
                                        (the app uses todo.md when both exist)

IMPORTANT - YOUR LIST IS PUBLIC
  Free GitHub Pages only works with a PUBLIC repository, so anyone who knows (or guesses)
  the web address can read todo.md. Don't put anything private in it: no passwords,
  account numbers, addresses, phone numbers, or confidential work details.
  Your ticks are NOT uploaded - they stay on your phone.

ONE-TIME SET-UP (about 5 minutes, on your computer)
  1. Sign in at github.com.
  2. Click + (top right) > New repository. Name it: to-do
     Choose PUBLIC. Click Create repository.
  3. Click "uploading an existing file". Drag in these 7 files:
     index.html, manifest.json, sw.js, icon-180.png, icon-192.png, icon-512.png, todo.md
     Click Commit changes.
  4. ADD THE .nojekyll FILE (needed so GitHub serves todo.md as plain text):
     In the repository click Add file > Create new file.
     Name it exactly:  .nojekyll      (starts with a dot; leave the content empty)
     Click Commit changes.
  5. Go to Settings > Pages. Under "Build and deployment", Source: Deploy from a branch,
     Branch: main, folder: / (root). Click Save.
  6. Wait 1-2 minutes. Your app is at:  https://<your-github-username>.github.io/to-do/

INSTALL ON YOUR IPHONE
  1. Open the address above in Safari.
  2. Tap Share > Add to Home Screen > Add.
  3. Always open it from the green To Do icon.

CHANGING YOUR LIST - RIGHT ON GITHUB (computer or iPhone)
  1. Go to github.com > your to-do repository > tap todo.md.
  2. Tap the pencil icon (Edit). On iPhone, if you don't see it, tap ... > Edit file.
  3. Make your changes. Tap Commit changes (and Commit changes again to confirm).
  4. Wait about a minute, then open the app (or tap the blue refresh button).
     A message shows how many tasks were added or removed.
  Tip: in Safari, Share > Add to Home Screen on the todo.md page too, so editing is one tap away.

HOW TO WRITE todo.md  (the same cheat-sheet is at the top of the file)
  # Home                          <- a list (each list is a page in the app)
  ## Bills                        <- optional section heading
  - Pay electricity bill | due 27/11 | high
  - Water the plants | x3 | note: Three times this week
  - Renew rego | due 8/12 | link: https://www.vicroads.vic.gov.au
  - Spring clean bedroom          <- steps on indented lines (2 spaces, then "- ")
    - Change sheets
    - Vacuum
  - Weekly shop | steps: Milk; Eggs; Bread

  Details after the task, separated by |  (any order, all optional):
    due 27/11  or  due 27/11/2026  or  due 27 Nov
    high  /  medium  /  low
    x3  (needs doing 3 times)
    note: any text
    link: web address
    steps: one; two; three
  To finish with a task for good, delete its line.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - The app checks for changes each time you open it. With no signal it shows the
    last list it downloaded.
  - Ticks are linked to the List + Section + Task wording. Rewording a task (or moving
    it under another heading) starts it fresh. Changing due date, priority, x3, steps,
    notes or links keeps the ticks.
  - Steps you add inside the app are saved on your phone only (not in todo.md).
  - If the app says "GitHub is turning todo.md into a web page", step 4 (.nojekyll)
    is missing.
  - Back up your ticks now and then: ••• > Backup & restore. Deleting the Home Screen
    icon deletes its ticks.
  - Needs iOS 16.4 or later.
